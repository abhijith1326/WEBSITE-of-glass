Glass Manufacturing

Glass Manufacturing
Step by Step

01
Raw Material
Batch Preparation & Melting
Premium silica sand (SiO₂ >99.5%), soda ash, limestone, and cullet are precisely weighed and blended in our automated batch plant. The mixture is fed into a 1,600°C float glass furnace where it melts into a homogeneous molten glass over 24+ hours.
Temperature: 1,600°CSilica Purity: 99.5%+Furnace Capacity: 600 TPD
✅ Batch composition verified by automated weighing systems with ±0.1% accuracy
02
Float Process
Tin Bath Float Formation
Molten glass flows onto a bath of molten tin. Because glass floats on tin and the surface tension creates a perfectly flat ribbon, this process produces glass of exceptional optical clarity and flatness. Ribbon thickness is precisely controlled from 2mm to 25mm.
Tin Bath: 600–1000°CFlatness: ≤0.1% distortionWidth: Up to 3,300mm
✅ Online optical distortion monitoring with camera systems
03
Annealing & Cooling
Controlled Cooling (Lehr)
The glass ribbon passes through the annealing lehr — a controlled cooling tunnel that eliminates internal stresses by slowly reducing temperature from 600°C to room temperature. Precise cooling rates ensure the glass is stress-free and dimensionally stable.
Lehr Length: 100m+Cooling Rate: ControlledExit Temp: 35–40°C
04
Inspection
Automated Quality Scanning
The continuous glass ribbon passes through our automated inspection line with high-resolution cameras and laser systems that detect inclusions, bubbles, scratches, thickness variations, and optical defects at speeds up to 400 m/minute.
Defect Detection: <0.3mmScan Speed: 400m/minAI-Assisted Analysis
✅ 100% of glass is scanned — no defective glass proceeds to cutting
05
Processing
CNC Cutting, Tempering & Processing
Float glass sheets are cut to size by CNC water-jet or diamond cutting heads, then processed as required: tempering (700°C heat treatment), laminating (PVB/SGP interlayer autoclave), coating (magnetron sputtering), drilling, edge working, and IGU assembly.
Tempering: 700°C+Autoclave: 14 bar / 140°CSputtering: 7-chamber
✅ Post-tempering fracture test on 100% of toughened glass
06
Final QC & Dispatch
Packaging & Secure Delivery
Each finished glass product undergoes final dimension check, visual inspection, and labeling before being packed in custom timber crates, A-frames, or purpose-built containers with vibration-dampening materials for safe transit to sites worldwide.
Custom CratingGPS Tracked ShipmentsWorldwide Delivery
✅ Every delivery comes with quality certificates and test reports
