Our Journey

Three Decades of
Building the Future

1989
The Beginning
Founded as a small glass cutting and processing unit in Cochin, Kerala, with 12 employees and a single flat-bed cutting machine. Supplied to local contractors and interior designers.
1997
Tempering Plant Commissioned
Installed our first toughened glass furnace, expanding capacity to 200 m² per day. Secured first large commercial contract — a 5-star hotel facade in Kochi.
2003
Plywood Division Launch
Diversified into premium plywood manufacturing, setting up a 50,000 sq.ft. plant in Perumbavoor. BWR and marine-grade products launched, achieving BIS certification.
2010
International Expansion
Established export operations to the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia. Received ISO 9001:2008 certification. Revenue crossed ₹500 Crore milestone.
2016
Smart Glass R&D Centre
Opened a dedicated R&D facility focusing on smart glass, electrochromic coatings, and low-E glass. Filed 8 patents in advanced glass processing.
2021
Sustainability Pledge
Committed to carbon neutrality by 2030. Installed 5 MW solar plant, implemented closed-loop water recycling, and launched green product certifications.
2024
Global Operations
Operating 4 manufacturing plants, 6 regional offices, and supply chains spanning 48 countries. Workforce of 2,800+ professionals dedicated to excellence.
