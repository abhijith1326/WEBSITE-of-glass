FAQ

Common
Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?+
We work with projects of all sizes. While there is no strict minimum, standard orders typically start from 50 m² for glass products. For specialty glass, please contact us for project-specific requirements.
How long does production and delivery take?+
Standard glass products are typically ready in 5–10 working days. Specialty glass (fire-rated, smart glass) may take 15–25 working days. International shipping timelines vary by destination — our logistics team will provide precise delivery schedules at the time of order.
Do you provide installation services?+
We provide technical installation guidance and can recommend certified installation partners in your region. For large projects, our project management team can oversee installation coordination.
What certifications do your products carry?+
Our products are certified to IS (Bureau of Indian Standards), EN (European Norms), ASTM (American Standards), and BS (British Standards) depending on the product type. We hold ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001, and OHSAS 18001 for our manufacturing operations.
Can you produce custom glass shapes and sizes?+
Yes. Our CNC processing equipment can cut any custom shape, radius, or complex geometry. We handle triangular, hexagonal, circular, curved, and any architectural form. Please share your drawings (DXF/DWG format preferred) for an accurate quotation.
